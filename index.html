📖 Game Description

This project recreates the frenetic, arcade-like essence of Metal Slug, the classic run-and-gun game by SNK. Specifically, we have recreated the first level of Metal Slug: Super Vehicle-001.

Players control a rebel soldier fighting through enemy battalions, armored vehicles, and giant bosses. The story follows the Peregrine Falcon Squad as they battle against General Morden and his army, who have taken control of the world.

The goal is to advance through action-packed levels, rescue prisoners of war, collect powerful weapons, and destroy everything in your path. With detailed pixel art graphics and fast-paced gameplay, our project is a tribute to the arcade gems of the 90s.

Full audio integration (pistol, grenade, machine gun, soldier death roulette, NPC rescue, jetpack pickup, boss fight, player death). Game states management (INTRO, TITLE, GAME). Blinking GO sign. Mikolaj Szeliga: Creation and rescaling of all sprites (Marco, backgrounds, items, enemies, grenades, explosions, bullets). Environment sprites (waterfall, water, huts, parachute, dust, smoke). Jetpack and boss sprites (phase 1, phase 2, debris). UI sprites (HUD, fonts, intro, tutorial). File organization and continuous sprite polishing.
